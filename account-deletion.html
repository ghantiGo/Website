Delete Your Account

Use this form to permanently delete your {{ site.data.site.app_name }} account: the resident app, the Partner app or the Camp app. You do not need the app installed. We send a code by text message to the number on the account, so only the owner of the number can delete it.

{% include deletion_form.html %}

One number can have an account in more than one app. Delete each one separately, by choosing its app above.

What Gets Deleted

When you confirm the code, the following is permanently deleted at once:

  • Your phone number, name and profile
  • Saved addresses and your chosen locality
  • Your requests to a peddler to stop at your society
  • For peddlers and camp organisers: your posts, plans, stops, bell rings, and the photos and voice recordings you uploaded
  • Your sign-in on every phone, and your notification token

Anything still held on our side is gone within {{ site.data.legal.account_deletion.processing_time }}. Once deleted, it cannot be reversed. You can register again later with the same number, as a new account.

What We Keep, and Why

  • Security and audit records, kept for {{ site.data.legal.data_retention.security_logs_days }} days (the minimum under the DPDP Rules 2025). Your name and number are already removed from them.
  • Usage and crash events, which are no longer linked to you. They follow the retention period in our Privacy Policy.
  • Database backups are overwritten on a 7-day cycle, so a deleted account can remain in an old backup for up to 7 days.

Captains, and Numbers You Have Lost

Captain accounts are set up by an administrator and cannot be deleted here. If you are a captain, or you no longer have the number on your account, email {{ site.data.legal.account_deletion.email }} from the address we have for you, with your name and the app, and we will help.

Contact Us

For questions about account deletion, contact {{ site.data.site.contact_email }}.
